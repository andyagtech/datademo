# USDS Data Engineering Take-Home Assessment

## Purpose

Suppose you wanted to replace a healthcare claims processing system with a new one. You want to make sure the outputs from the new system match the old system before proceeding. Build a small, reliable data comparison and validation tool that compares the outcomes of the two claims processing systems. The data from the new system intentionally contains issues and inconsistencies, and your goal is to surface them, quantify their impact, identify any trends you see, and communicate what they mean for accuracy. Use the language and database you think are most appropriate to the task. There should be a way for users to review the results, it could be an excel, charts, basic web view, tableau or whatever medium you find best communicates your findings. Be prepared to explain your code and design decisions. For the initial assessment your depth of analysis, analysis strategy, and supporting documentation providing evidence of your work will be key factors in advancing you to the next round of interviews.

We designed all components of this assignment to be completed between 4 and 6 hours, excluding setup, but please take more time if needed.
